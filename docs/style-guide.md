# Design System: Liquid Glass

## Creative North Star
"The Digital Alchemist"
This system relies on deep tonal depth, high-contrast serif typography, and a deliberate rejection of traditional structural lines.

## The "No-Line" Rule
1px solid borders are strictly prohibited for sectioning. Boundaries must be defined solely through background color shifts or subtle tonal transitions.

## Colors
- **surface:** `#131313`
- **surface-container-lowest:** `#141414`
- **surface-container-low:** `#1b1b1b`
- **surface-container:** `#1f1f1f`
- **surface-container-high:** `#272727`
- **surface-container-highest:** `#353535`
- **primary:** `#ddb7ff`
- **secondary:** `#cdbdff`
- **secondary_container:** `#5b00f0`

## Typography
- **Display & Headline:** Playfair Display, Noto Serif
- **Functional:** Inter
- **Technical/Tags:** Space Grotesk

## Shadows & Depth
Use **Ambient Shadows**: Blur 40-60px, Opacity 4-8%, tinted with `secondary` (#cdbdff) or `primary`. Avoid pure black drop shadows. Use ring-1 ring-white/5 for "Ghost Borders" if necessary.
